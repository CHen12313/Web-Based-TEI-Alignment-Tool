Eochaid Bélbuide, son of Feidlimid Rechtmar, was Conn's brother. He went
                    into Ulster under safeguard, to escape from his brother Conn, for Eochaid was
                    ill-bred and unruly, and was destroying his brother's rule and authority. Then,

What Tibraide did was to go to Scotland, to the king of Scotland, Failbe
                    Findloga, and he was three years with him. Then the king of Scotland advised him
                    to come to Ireland and make peace with Conn. It was all done thus. The Ulstermen
                    bid him be at peace with Conn. He said  to make peace?, but he did not venture to come to Conn under
